Transaction Guidelines
  • Deposit: Amount will be added to the selected account.
  • Withdrawal: Amount will be deducted from the selected account.
  • Transfer: Destination account is required.
  • Only active accounts can perform transactions.
  • Balance changes occur only for successful transactions.
